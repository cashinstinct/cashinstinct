About and methodology

About Cash Instinct

Cash Instinct brings scattered information together to help Canadian consumers understand an offer, its important terms and its limitations before signing up or paying.

Why Cash Instinct exists

Details about a single offer are often spread across several places: the official page, program terms, FAQs, account screens and public discussions. Cash Instinct organizes the useful pieces into guides that are easier to consult and grounded in the Canadian context.

The goal is not to replace official terms. It is to highlight what deserves attention before a sign-up, purchase or payment so readers can avoid preventable surprises.

How the guides are prepared

  • Official program pages and terms are prioritized.
  • Limitations, exclusions, deadlines and eligibility rules are actively checked and explained.
  • Screenshots, examples or FAQs are added when they provide evidence or make a process easier to understand.
  • Relevant sources and verification dates are made visible in the guides.

Documentation and personal experience

Not every program featured on Cash Instinct has been personally tested. First-hand experience is identified clearly when it exists. Otherwise, the guide is presented as documented research based on the sources available.

This distinction separates personal observations from rules published by a provider.

Tools and original work

Cash Instinct does more than list offers. The site also develops resources designed to support a decision or calculation:

  • the points value calculator;
  • the RHT method guide;
  • Internet provider comparisons;
  • step-by-step guides based on real sign-up and usage flows.

Affiliation and independence

Some links are referral or affiliate links. Using one may allow Cash Instinct to receive a reward at no extra cost to the reader.

An affiliation does not prevent the site from presenting unfavourable terms, limitations, exclusions or a program suspension. A paused offer must not be presented as active.

Report a correction

If you notice a factual error, broken link or change in a program’s terms, you can open an issue in the Cash Instinct GitHub repository.

This channel is used to track technical and editorial corrections. Support requests concerning the services featured on the site should be directed to the relevant provider.

Offers and updates

Amounts, eligibility rules and timelines can change. Before signing up, check the verification date shown in the guide and confirm the current terms on the program’s official website.
