Quickly compare the timing window, rate, and critical step for each Canadian program.

Updated August 6, 2026

About this method
The Refundable Hotel Trick (RHT) is a community strategy based on how rewards programs operate and on the experience of many users. Banks do not document this technique or guarantee its results. Official rules—eligibility, timing, exclusions, and required portals—are cited when available, while the method's steps come from practical community experience. Programs can change; use this information at your own risk.

Scene+

Scene+ Rewards (Scotiabank)

Official window: up to one year after the travel purchase.

Scene+ lets members apply points to eligible travel purchases at 1¢ per point. Review the official Scene+ guidance before redeeming.

  1. Book a refundable hotel room with your Scotiabank credit card (e.g., Scotia Gold Amex).
  2. Log into your Scotia account, navigate to your transactions, and select the "Apply Points to Travel" option.
  3. Select the eligible hotel transaction, up to one year after purchase, and apply the desired amount of points.
  4. The statement credit will appear within the following business days.
  5. Once the points credit has processed, cancel with the merchant so the refund returns to the Card.
MBNA Rewards

MBNA Rewards

Window: confirm the eligibility and timing shown in the portal.

MBNA values travel redemptions at 100 points per dollar. Its public terms do not guarantee a fixed 90-day retroactive window; review the official MBNA Rewards page, then open the secure rewards portal from your account.

  1. Book a fully refundable hotel on any booking site (Expedia, Booking, or directly with the hotel) with your MBNA card.
  2. Wait for the transaction to change from pending to posted.
  3. Log into the MBNA Rewards portal and confirm that the purchase appears as an eligible travel expense.
  4. Apply your points against the hotel purchase. The statement credit will be applied within a few days.
  5. Once the points credit has processed, cancel with the merchant so the refund returns to the Card.
BMO Rewards

BMO Rewards

Official window: posted transaction, up to 90 days.

BMO lets members apply points to eligible travel purchases at 150 points per dollar, or about 0.67¢ per point. Review the official BMO Rewards information.

  1. Charge a refundable hotel transaction to your BMO Rewards card.
  2. Within a maximum of 90 days, log into the BMO Rewards portal and select "Pay with Points".
  3. Apply your points against this travel transaction. The credit will post to your account within 3 to 5 days.
  4. Once the points credit has processed, cancel with the merchant so the refund returns to the Card.
À la carte

NBC À la carte Rewards

Window: posted transaction, up to 60 days.

NBC lets members apply a travel discount to eligible purchases within 60 days. The value is 12,000 points per $100, or 11,000 points per $100 for an eligible redemption of 55,000 points or more. Review the official National Bank instructions.

  1. Book a refundable hotel on a third-party site (Expedia, Booking, etc.) so the transaction is categorized as a travel purchase by Mastercard.
  2. Wait for the transaction to officially post to your account (not pending).
  3. Within 60 days following the purchase, log into the NBC Rewards portal, go to the travel section, and select "Flexible travel discount".
  4. Apply your points against this specific expense. The statement credit will be issued within a few days.
  5. Once the travel credit has processed, cancel with the merchant so the refund returns to the Card.
Aventura

CIBC Aventura

Very short window: act while the transaction is pending.

CIBC's official Shopping with Points feature lets members apply points only to an eligible transaction that has not yet posted. The rate is the one displayed by CIBC at redemption. Review the official CIBC instructions and terms.

  1. Book a fully refundable hotel through a third party (Expedia, Booking, etc.). Never go through the CIBC portal.
  2. Monitor your account: as soon as the transaction appears as "Pending", the "Redeem with points" option activates.
  3. Apply your points immediately against this transaction. The option will disappear once the transaction officially posts.
  4. Once the points payment has processed, cancel through the third party; numerous consistent reports from Canadian cardholders indicate that the refund then returns to the Card.
Error to avoid: do not book directly with points through the CIBC travel portal. Cancelling that type of booking normally returns the value as Aventura points instead of a refund to the Card.
Repeated community observations: CIBC rates and promotions
The commonly observed Shopping with Points rate is 1¢ per point, and some promotions have offered roughly 1.25¢. CIBC determines the ratio and may change it without notice, so always verify the displayed rate before confirming.
Annual Credit

Amex Gold / Platinum Travel Credit

Community method: refundable prepaid booking through Amex Travel.

The annual credit is $100 for the Gold Rewards Card and $200 for the Platinum Card, applied to one eligible booking of at least the same amount. Review the official Amex travel-credit rules.

  1. Open Amex Travel Canada, sign in, and choose an eligible booking of at least $100 or $200, depending on the Card.
  2. At checkout, select the available Annual Travel Credit; the full amount is charged to the Card and the credit normally appears within two business days.
  3. Wait for the Annual Travel Credit to post, then cancel in accordance with the supplier's fare rules.
  4. The refund normally returns to the Card. Based on numerous user reports, the Annual Travel Credit remains used and is not automatically clawed back, but this outcome is not guaranteed.
TD Rewards

TD First Class Travel (FCT)

Advanced community method: product switch before cancellation.

TD officially lets members redeem at 200 points per dollar on Expedia for TD. The product-switch sequence below is widely reported by Canadian travel-rewards enthusiasts. Also review the official TD instructions.

  1. Book a refundable hotel through the Expedia for TD (E4TD) portal to get the maximum rate.
  2. Apply your TD Rewards points to cover the transaction.
  3. Critical step: request a product switch from the FCT card to a TD card that does not use TD Rewards, such as a Cash Back card.
  4. Wait until the switch is fully completed in TD's systems, then cancel the booking. According to repeated reports from Canadian cardholders, without the original TD Rewards account the value may be credited in cash to the new Card.
Error to avoid: cancelling before the product switch is complete normally sends the points back to the TD Rewards account. Results still vary (YMMV), and TD does not guarantee the outcome.
Logistics & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Refundable Hotel Trick (RHT)?

The Refundable Hotel Trick (RHT) is a travel-rewards cash-out method where you book a refundable hotel, redeem points or a travel credit against that charge, then cancel the booking so the refund returns to your card in cash.

Is the Refundable Hotel Trick allowed by banks?

Rewards programs do not document the RHT as a cash-out method. Cancelling after using points or a travel credit may be treated as abuse, lead to a benefit reversal, or trigger an account review. Always check the current official terms.

Do all Canadian rewards programs work the same way?

No. Some programs require the hotel charge to be posted before you redeem points, while others only work while the transaction is still pending.

What should you do with a credit balance?

If your cash-out generates a large positive balance, you can either spend it organically on daily expenses (recommended) or contact the financial institution to request a check for the excess balance.

Can you use the RHT with travel credits instead of points?

Sometimes. For example, some cardholders use refundable prepaid hotel bookings to trigger annual travel credits, then cancel the reservation after the credit appears.
