Method

A monthly price is not the whole story

A 24-month promotion, a permanent price and a gift card cannot be compared correctly in one price column. The engine keeps each element separate, then calculates net cost over the deadline you choose.

promotional months × promotional price + remaining months × regular price + one-time fees + equipment − credits

Important: net cost can be negative when the credit you enter exceeds the costs during the modeled period. That means the model contains more credits than charges; it is not a promise of payment.

How to use it

Four steps to a more useful comparison

1. Copy the offers

Record the promotional or current price, promotional length, post-promotion rate, installation or activation fees, and any monthly equipment charge.

2. Keep credits separate

Enter a gift card, account credit or one-time discount in its own field only after checking eligibility and when it is applied.

3. Choose your deadline

The 12, 24 and 36-month buttons are shortcuts. A custom period can match a move, the end of a promotion or another real deadline.

4. Read beyond the total

Total and effective monthly cost help with the decision, but download, upload, technology and service conditions can matter more for your household.

Sources and limits

What official sources help you verify

The CRTC Internet Code explains how to present promotional and post-promotion prices, as well as one-time fees. The CRTC page on Internet performance and reliability defines download and upload speeds and notes that equipment, the home network and the address affect the experience.

These sources do not say which plan is best for you. They support the tool's structure; the values shown in the calculator come from your own contracts, carts and checks.

Frequently asked questions

About the calculator

What calculation does it use?
Total cost is promotional months multiplied by the promotional price, plus remaining months multiplied by the regular price, plus one-time fees and equipment, minus credits or gift cards. Effective monthly cost is the total divided by the selected duration.
Why keep credits or gift cards separate from the monthly price?
A credit or gift card is a one-time benefit with its own conditions; it is not a permanent reduction in the monthly price. The separate field makes its effect visible and lets you remove it if eligibility is not confirmed.
Should I enter taxes in the calculator?
The calculator does not determine taxes. Enter comparable amounts according to how the offers display their prices, then verify the total and fees in the provider's contract or critical information summary.
What does post-promotion price mean?
It is the monthly price used after the promotional period. If you leave the field blank, the calculator reuses the promotional or current price; that works for a permanent price, but enter the regular rate when it differs.
Does this tool recommend a provider?
No. It compares the values you enter and assigns no arbitrary score. Speed, upload, technology, availability, service and offer conditions still need to be checked for your address.
